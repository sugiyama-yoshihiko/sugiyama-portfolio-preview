Sugiyama Yoshihiko

Short statement

My practice begins with ceramics and extends to the making of everyday lives and places to belong. Moving between Mie, Aichi, Tokyo, and Fukushima also informs my interest in movement, settlement, and the making of places to belong.

Using tin cans repurposed as kilns, I question the dependence of pottery-making on specialised facilities and explore how making can be shared and renewed through relationships.

Across the Katsurao-yaki Project in Fukushima, the Banana Pottery Club in Aichi, and my direction of the art space NANJI in Mie, I seek to leave behind not just objects or one-off experiences, but relationships and conditions that allow making to continue. I call the spaces that emerge through people, places, and accumulated time “scenes,” and my practice of bringing them into being “scene-making.”
