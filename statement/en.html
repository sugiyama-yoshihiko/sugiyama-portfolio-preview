Statement

Beginning with ceramics, my practice explores how we can build everyday lives and places to belong with our own hands.

Moving between micro- and macro-scale practices centered on ceramics, I seek a sense of being alive by creating “comfortable everyday lives” with our own hands, and by rethinking and remaking everyday life and society itself.

And my life moving between Mie, Aichi, Tokyo, and Fukushima has also led me to explore movement and settlement, everyday life, and the making of places to belong. When I consider activities such as Katsurao-yaki as artworks, I see the community as their smallest unit. Working from the hypothesis that a hut may be the material expression of that community, I use houses and small shrines as motifs.
